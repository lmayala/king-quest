=== STAGE 3: THE PATHS OF THE DEAD ===

Aragorn descends beneath the mountain into realms of shadow. To claim his birthright there he needs two things: one is secret and powerful (an oath-token), and the other is open knowledge shared among all who know how to speak.

In Kubernetes terms, this is what **Secrets** and **ConfigMaps** provide. A ConfigMap holds configuration data available to everyone in that realm. A Secret holds sensitive information that must stay hidden. Both can be mounted into a Pod so a scout carries them as it travels.

OBJECTIVE:

Carry hidden intelligence and open commands through the Paths of the Dead. You need both forms.

REQUIRED SETUP:

Namespace: 'gondor' (already exists)
Secret: 'secret-oath-token' key: token → value: barahir-ring
ConfigMap: 'configmap-words-of-calling' key: words → value: summon-the-dead
Deployment: 'beacon-palantir-army' image: nginx, replicas: 1

Your scout must carry the label 'app=beacon-palantir' so it can be discovered by name.

GUIDANCE:

1. Create the Secret using kubectl create secret with --from-literal to store the oath-token value. The Secret lives inside the gondor namespace. This is the confidential power Aragorn needs.

2. Create the ConfigMap using kubectl create configmap with --from-literal to store the words of calling. This ConfigMap also lives inside the gondor namespace. Anyone in that realm may read these words.

3. Create a Deployment named 'beacon-palantir-army' in the gondor namespace. Your Pod template must have these labels:
   - app: beacon-palantir
   - The ConfigMap mounted as a volume at /etc/calling
   - The Secret injected as environment variable OATH_TOKEN

4. Inspect the scout with 'kubectl describe deployment beacon-palantir-army -n gondor'. In the Events section, you should see references to the Secret and ConfigMap. Run 'kubectl get pods -n gondor -l app=beacon-palantir' to verify it exists.

5. Execute a command on the scout to verify its cargo. Run: kubectl exec <pod-name> -n gondor -- cat /etc/calling/words to confirm the ConfigMap was mounted. Run: kubectl exec <pod-name> -n gondor -- env | grep OATH_TOKEN to confirm the Secret was injected.

When the scout runs in the gondor realm carrying both cargo, run 'check_paths_of_dead' to report to Aragorn that he has his equipment for the journey below.
