The Wisdom of Galadriel: Secrets and Guidance

The Fellowship, weary from their journey through Moria, finds refuge in the mystical realm of Lothlórien, the golden 
wood. Here, they are welcomed by Galadriel, the Lady of Light, whose wisdom and foresight are unparalleled.

Galadriel offers guidance and a glimpse into the hearts of the Fellowship. She tests their resolve and reveals the 
hidden strengths and fears within them. In this realm of ancient power and subtle enchantments, understanding what is 
revealed and what must remain hidden becomes crucial.

Your task is to help establish a secure way for Galadriel's wisdom (her message) and her more hidden insights (secrets) 
to be managed, ensuring that only those meant to access them can do so.
