=== STAGE 7: THE FIRES OF MOUNT DOOM ===

The Ring has been carried to the Cracks of Doom. The final act is not
a battle — it is a single command that must complete and never repeat.

In Kubernetes, a **Job** is work that runs to completion and stops.
A **CronJob** repeats on a schedule. The destruction of the Ring
requires both: one final act, and an eternal watch.

OBJECTIVE:

Create the final work in the realm of Mordor.

REQUIRED SETUP:

1. Create the namespace `mordor` (if it does not exist).

2. **Job: `cast-ring`**
   - Runs exactly once, writes a record of the Ring's destruction
   - Container: `busybox`, command writes `"THE RING IS DESTROYED"` to `/end/record.txt`
   - Must use `restartPolicy: Never`
   - Must have `backoffLimit: 0` (no retries — the Ring is destroyed once, or not at all)
   - Job name: `destroy-ring`

3. **CronJob: `ring-watcher`**
   - Runs every minute (`* * * * *`)
   - Creates a Job that appends a timestamped line to `/var/log/mount-doom.log`
   - CronJob must have `successfulJobsHistoryLimit: 3`, `failedJobsHistoryLimit: 1`
   - CronJob name: `ring-watcher`

4. **Verification Record**
   - The Job `destroy-ring` must have `backoffLimit: 0` (no retries)
   - The CronJob `ring-watcher` must have `successfulJobsHistoryLimit: 3`
   - Both resources must exist in namespace `mordor`

GUIDANCE:

- Create namespace: `kubectl create namespace mordor`
- Create Job YAML with `restartPolicy: Never`, `backoffLimit: 0`
- Create CronJob YAML with `schedule: "* * * * *"`, `successfulJobsHistoryLimit: 3`, `failedJobsHistoryLimit: 1`
- Use `kubectl apply -f` to create both resources
- Verify Job completes with `kubectl get job destroy-ring -n mordor`
- Watch CronJob create Jobs: `kubectl get jobs -n mordor -w`
- Check logs of completed pods: `kubectl logs <pod-name> -n mordor`

When both resources exist and the Job has completed successfully,
run `check_mount_doom` to verify the Ring's destruction.
