The Tower of Cirith Ungol stands silent on the rim of Mordor. Sam has hidden Frodo in a dark corner of the fortress, placed a shard of Elanor in his hand, and whispered words of hope before seeking help.

But now Sam has gone ahead to scout — and behind him, something shifts. An orc guard, drawn by the silence, investigates the hiding place. A sword. A scream. The pod dies.

When Sam returns, panicked, breath caught in his throat — the Elfstone is still there. The words are still carved upon the wall. Both exact. Both unchanged.

"I will come back," Sam had sworn. And he did — not to emptiness, but to the same small fortress. The stone remembers. The hope endures.

That is what you build now: a keep that returns even after disaster. Not a passing patrol — a permanent stronghold. Not ephemeral volunteers — a structural promise written in stone.
