The plains before the Black Gate are silent. But within,
the enemies maneuver — sentries slipping through shadow,
soldiers falling in the wastes. The Captain cannot be everywhere,
but he knows which watchmen still stand and which have vanished
from the line.

Some soldiers pretend to serve, but inside the fort there is no
life. The darkness has consumed them. A sentry who falls is
heard. A silent sentinel is invisible to the watchful eye.

At the Black Gate, both kinds of failure must be monitored:
the sentry who has fallen (external probe finds no one there),
and the sentry who stands but cannot yet receive commands
(internal confusion must be detected early).

This is the work of Liveness Probes and Readiness Probes —
the first tells of death in combat, the second tells of confusion
before taking the field. And when the enemy grows stronger,
more guards appear automatically from the reserve lines.

The Black Gate is prepared. The wall holds, the scouts stand
vigilant, and the army grows when the threat grows. Now you
teach you how to fight as one — and shift the tide back east.
