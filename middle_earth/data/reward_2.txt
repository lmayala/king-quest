organized under Araldh of the Marksman — their Deployment,
ensuring no company is left behind.

The Muster is complete. Every scout reports ready.

Now send word to Gondor. Tell Denethor: Rohan marches east.
Tell Aragorn: the Rohirrim are organized. Tell the beacon-scouts:
they will be needed soon.

For ahead lies the road through Moria — where numbers alone cannot
survive. Scaling an army matters when the enemy waits inside.
But you must learn first how to reach the depths.

Prepare your command structure. Your forces will soon stretch
far beyond what fits in a single cluster.
