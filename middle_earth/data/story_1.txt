Isengard is drowned. The furnaces are silent, the forges cold. But victory here did not break the Shadow — only hardened its gaze. Through the Palantír, Sauron turned his eyes east, toward Minas Tirith. Far beyond the White City, Aragorn walks the Paths of the Dead, claiming his birthright in blood and oath. Meanwhile, Denethor holds the throne. Not a king — a Steward, ruling in place of the absent heir. There is no king because Dúnedain walked away centuries ago, and their last wanderer is far from home. But there does not need to be: the Steward governs, the gates hold fast, and Minas Tirith stands alone. Gondor has no king. Gondor needs no king.

From the heights of Minas Tirith, the first flame rises — a column of fire piercing the gathering dark. Amon Dîn answers. Eilenach. Nardol. Erelas. Min-Rimmon. Calenhad. Halifirien.

Seven beacons across the border mountains, calling the Rohirrim to honour their ancient oath. "The beacons of Gondor are lit! Gondor calls for aid!" "And Rohan will answer. Muster the Rohirrim!"

You must build the realms and ignite the fires. Let the beacons burn.
