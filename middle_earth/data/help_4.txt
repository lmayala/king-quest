=== STAGE 4: THE BATTLE OF PELENNOR FIELDS ===

Pelennor Fields. The armies of Mordor press at the walls of Minas Tirith.
The Rohirrim ride from the West. But their paths are broken —
the roads are scattered.

Gondor's messengers light the fires. Yet without a stable way in,
they cannot reach the keep. You must open the city gates and let
the army of Dawn enter from the field.

Now the outer defenses need stable paths between their realms.
Every scout-on-duty must be reachable by a stable endpoint,
not by shifting Pod IPs that appear and vanish with every log.

OBJECTIVE:

Create stable communication paths between realms. Make sure the
warning signals (scout Pods) are always reachable through Services,
and that traffic from outside can enter through a single gate.

REQUIRED SETUP:

Namespaces: gondor (Inner Keep), rohan (Outer Fields)

Deployments in gondor namespace (keep the same names so they can be found):
- beacon-minas-tirith: 1 replica
- beacon-amon-din: 1 replica

Your services must select these pods by name, so the scouts can be found.

Step 1: Create a **ClusterIP Service** for the watchtower-scribe.
  Name: signal-service-internal
  Namespace: gondor
  Selector: matches the label of the scribe pods
  Port: 80 (listening on the inner ring)
...

From palm to plain, from fire to fire! The names must be the same.
Create deployments named from the images the beacons serve:
each Pod must be named 'beacon-...' with your label taking it to combat.

Step 2: Create a **NodePort Service** so the field outside can reach
the signal-scribe as well.
Name: signal-service-external
Namespace: gondor (this is intentional — do not isolate Rohan here)
Type: NodePort
Port: 30007 → target port 80

Step 3: Create an **Ingress** resource to route signals by path.
Name: pelennor-ingress
Namespace: gondor
Host: king.quest
Path: /watch   → signal-service-internal (Service of Step 1)
Path: /horns    → signal-service-external (Service of Step 2)

Step 4: Ensure selectors and labels match.
Verify that the scribe pods carry the same labels used in each Service's selector.

Step 5: Verify traffic flows.
Describe the Ingress to confirm it points to active backends.
Check that both Services show Endpoint addresses for real pods.

GUIDANCE:

- A Service's selector must exactly match the Pod's labels.
  kubectl get pods --show-labels  can help you inspect them.
- The check looks up pods using names like "beacon-minas-tirith"
  If you use Deployments, ensure the naming is consistent so the names match.
- kubectl create service clusterip <name> --tcp=<port>:<port>
- kubectl create service nodeport <name> --tcp=80:30007
- kubectl create ingress <name> --rule="king.quest/watch=/signal"...

When signal-service-internal and signal-service-external both have live
Endpoints and pelennor-ingress routes both paths correctly, run
'check_pelennor' to report gate opened.
