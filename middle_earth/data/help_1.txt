=== STAGE 1: THE LIGHTING OF THE BEACONS ===

Gondor's Steward Denethor demands his vassals prepare their strongholds.
But first, he must create the realms these commanders will govern.
Each domain must exist separately: no confusion between Gondor and Rohan.
These are your Namespaces. They are the foundation of order in a large kingdom.

From the heights of Minas Tirith, the first flame rises,
a column of fire piercing the gathering dark.
Amon Dîn answers. Eilenach. Nardol. Erelas.
Min-Rimmon. Calenhad. Halifirien.

Seven beacons across the border mountains, calling the Rohirrim
to honour their ancient oath. Rohan's king Théoden sends word back
from the plains: send scouts to each realm to report that preparations
are underway. These scouts are your Pods. They carry information back
to the Steward.

OBJECTIVE:

Set up two isolated realms and dispatch named and labeled scouts to each one.
In Gondor, every mountain peak needs its own beacon, eight in total,
one for Minas Tirith and seven for the signal chain. In Rohan, one
scout marks where the Rohirrim muster.

To organize our tracking, the Steward demands that every scout Pod be
explicitly tagged with a label identifying its faction:
- Gondor scouts must have the label: 'realm=gondor'
- Rohan scouts must have the label:   'realm=rohan'

REQUIRED SETUP:

Gondor namespace ('gondor') — 8 scouts (all with label 'realm=gondor'):
  - 'beacon-minas-tirith'   (the White City, where the Steward rules)
  - 'beacon-amon-din'       (highest peak, first to see the enemy)
  - 'beacon-eilenach'       (westernmost watchpost)
  - 'beacon-nardol'         (crossings of the Poros)
  - 'beacon-erelas'         (field of Lebethron trees)
  - 'beacon-min-rimmon'     (eastern sentinel on the Meringue)
  - 'beacon-calenhad'       (the Great Plain overlooking the Entwash)
  - 'beacon-halifirien'     (holy hill where the oath was sworn)

Rohan namespace ('rohan') — 1 scout (with label 'realm=rohan'):
  - 'beacon-edoras'          (capital of the Mark, far across Anórien)

Total: 9 scouts across 2 namespaces.

GUIDANCE:

- Use 'kubectl get <resource>' to understand what exists before you act.
  This is how every operator learns their system.

- Every resource has a namespace, if you don't specify one, it goes to
  'default'. Creating resources in separate namespaces keeps everything
  properly isolated.

- Use 'kubectl run --help' to discover options for creating Pods.
  You will need the '--labels' flag (e.g. '--labels=realm=gondor') to tag them.
  Try '--dry-run=client' to preview without committing anything first.
  Create scouts one by one, placing each into its correct namespace.

- When something isn't working as expected, 'kubectl describe <resource>'
  shows events that explain what went wrong.

When all nine scouts are active and correctly labeled in their respective
realms, run 'check_beacons' to report the Steward's success.
