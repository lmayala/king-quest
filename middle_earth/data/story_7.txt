The Black Gate breaks. Now nothing stands between you and the Fire. Frodo and Sam walk the final mile to the Crack of Doom. The Ring must be destroyed — not by armies, but by a single, decisive act carried to its end.

This is the last work of war. Not defense. Not preparation. Completion.

The Ring must be cast into the Cracks of Doom. It cannot be carried further, only finished. The tool for such a task is not an army that stands guard, but a command issued once and executed to completion. A Job that runs once, finishes, and never repeats. A CronJob that watches the aftermath, ensuring the silence holds.

This lesson is learned: some work must end. And when it ends, the record of its ending must persist.

But the war's end is not the story's end. The King returns. The Crown awaits.
