The Ring is gone. The Enemy is fallen. The Black Gate collapses.
Minas Tirith stands quiet under a new sunrise.

Aragorn stands before the White Tree. The crown of Elendil is
brought forth. The standard of the King unfurls. The city does
not just cheer — it organizes. A kingdom must be governed.

What was built for war must now serve peace. The infrastructure
that held the line must now serve the realm: configurable,
repeatable, versioned. The tools of war become the tools of
governance.

Helm charts. Values files. Releases. Rollbacks. The King's
decrees, codified and deployable across every realm of the
Reunited Kingdom.

This is the final lesson: infrastructure that served war
must now serve peace — and it must be manageable by those
who come after.
