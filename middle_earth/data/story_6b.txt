The Defense of Helm's Deep: Orchestrating Allied Forces

The Fellowship stands at the precipice of a great battle. The fortress of Helm's Deep is soon to be besieged, and every 
defense must be meticulously coordinated. From the command post within the Keep to the vigilant guards on the outer 
walls, and the scouts reporting from the surrounding lands, a robust and orchestrated system is needed to manage the 
defense.

In this critical moment, the ability to manage multiple defense points simultaneously, ensure their readiness, and 
maintain constant communication is paramount. Your task is to help build this complex defensive network, orchestrating 
the various components to withstand the coming storm.
