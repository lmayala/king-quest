Aragorn descends beneath the mountain into realms of shadow. To claim his birthright there he needs two things: one is secret and powerful (an oath-token), and the other is open knowledge shared among all who know how to speak.

In the deep places, a Secret hides — the barahir ring, bearer of an ancient oath. Only those who hold this token may call the Dead. Some power must remain hidden.

But above that, a ConfigMap holds the words of calling. These are not secret — any servant of the King may read them. Open knowledge, available to anyone in the realm.

Both forms carry weight: one concealed in darkness, one spoken aloud at dawn. Together they move armies beyond the grave.

The fellowship carries its intelligence both ways now. When Aragorn returns from the Paths of the Dead, his command structure will be complete.
