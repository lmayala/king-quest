The Bridge of Khazad-dûm

Deep in the darkness of Moria, the Fellowship reaches the great chasm before the Bridge of Khazad-dûm. As they cross, 
a terrible presence emerges — a Balrog of Morgoth, a demon of the ancient world, wreathed in shadow and flame.

Gandalf stands alone on the bridge to face it. "You shall not pass!" he cries. The Balrog advances, its whip of flame 
lashing out. With a mighty blow, Gandalf shatters the bridge beneath it, but as the Balrog falls, its whip lashes 
around Gandalf's ankle, dragging him into the abyss.

"Fly, you fools!" are his last words as he plunges into the darkness.
