The beacons burn! Seven fires light the border mountains, and from the western plains comes the answer: drums of war, the cry of horned helmets.

Théoden King rides to Dunharrow. From every vale and hillside, riders converge — ten thousand strong, gathered in a single muster. But an army without order is no army at all. Each regiment must stand ready: ready to march, ready to fight, ready to replace any who fall before the journey begins.

Araldh, captain of the marksman, stands with his company. One leader governs them all, yet each soldier fights as their own. If one drops from exhaustion, another takes their place — silently, seamlessly, while the muster continues.

This is what a Deployment gives you: declaration over chaos. You say how many scouts you need, and the system keeps that number steady. It does not matter how many fall; the muster endures.

Ten thousand riders will ride to Minas Tirith. First, organize the camp. Then, send word to Gondor: Rohan is ready.
