The armies of Mordor stand before Minas Tirith. Hundreds of spears
at the ready, thousands of orcs, trolls and siege-engines lining
up in the fields below. But an army of one thousand scouts can be
overwhelmed by the enemy's numbers.

To survive, the defenders must find a way to balance their forces
across every watch-point. No single gate can hold them all forever.
The beacons are burning. The rohirrim are ready. But unless the
alarms are shared across all seven gates, the city falls.

Your task: spread the defense across multiple scouts. Ensure that
if one falls, the signal still reaches Minas Tirith. Scale your
formations to handle the incoming tide, or the walls will fall.

This is the final test before the last charge: you must prove that
Minas Tirith can endure long enough for the King to return.
