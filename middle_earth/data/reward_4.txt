The gates of Minas Tirith stand open.
The beacons' light has drawn Rohan into the field.
From the heights, Denethor sees the Rohirrim approach —
a vast line of horse and steel riding out of the dawn.

Scout halls answer the call. The signal-scribes of Minas Tirith
are no longer isolated. They can summon aid from Lapundel and Amon Hen,
Cythera and the beacon-line — every name will be inscribed upon the wall.

You have learned to bind isolated embers into a burning line,
using names that survive the fall of any single watch.

But Sauron's eye has not dulled. Ahead lies deeper peril —
characters who cannot be killed once spoken aloud, and armies that can
scale up endlessly if not starved of life support.

Gather your force. Review your architecture.
The next stage opens a heavier gate.
