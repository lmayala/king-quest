The Breaking of the Fellowship

The Fellowship's journey reaches its crux at Amon Hen, the Seat of Hearing. As they row down the Anduin, Frodo feels 
the weight of the Ring growing unbearable. At Parth Galen, the Fellowship fractures — Boromir succumbs to the Ring's 
temptation and tries to take it from Frodo, who flees into the woods.

Frodo puts on the Ring atop Amon Hen, and for a moment, the Eye of Sauron sees him. The Fellowship scatters: Merry and 
Pippin are captured by orcs, Boromir falls defending them, and the remaining members are driven apart by fate.

It is the end of the Fellowship as it was known — but from its breaking, new paths will emerge. The quest continues 
not as nine, but as separate threads weaving the fate of Middle-earth.
