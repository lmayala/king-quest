The Ring is gone. The Enemy is fallen. The Black Gate collapses. Minas Tirith is no longer threatened.

The crown is placed upon Aragorn's head. The Age of the Ring has ended. The age of rebuilding begins.

But a kingdom must be governed by more than just a king's decree. The realm needs systems, governed by rules, that can be managed, updated, and shared across generations. Just as the Crown must be renewed, so too must the systems that support it.

Helm charts, repositories, deployments — these are the tools of the new age. The King's Grabaciones must be codified, versioned, and managed just like any other system. This is the final lesson: you must govern what you have built, not just dispose of it, but govern it wisely.
